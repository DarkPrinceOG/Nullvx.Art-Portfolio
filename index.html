02 // THE ARTIST

Behind Nullvx

I am a 2D visual artist working across traditional paper and digital rendering. My work focuses primarily on dark fantasy aesthetics, expressive anime and manga characters, and human sketch studies.

Rather than presenting only polished results, Nullvx exists to document the raw creative process from the ground up. Whether working with graphite pencils and ink on paper or drawing with standard digital tools, every piece reflects hours of disciplined practice and study.

01 / MEDIUMS

2D Digital & Traditional Graphite Paper

Seamless interplay between tactile tooth on heavyweight paper and pressure-sensitive digital stylus strokes.

02 / FOCUS

Dark Fantasy & Character Art

Exploring visceral mood, dark silhouettes, emotive facial expressions, and dynamic character anatomy.

03 / CRAFT

100% Hand-Drawn

Every mark, contour, cross-hatch, and render is composed stroke-by-stroke through authentic artistic discipline.
